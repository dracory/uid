Short IDs (Time-Ordered, High Throughput)

ShortID() 11 Chars

Crockford Base32 encoded microsecond timestamp with 4-bit rolling counter.

-
SafeShortID() 14 Chars

Vowel-free Base16 encoding (0-9, bcdfgh) to avoid accidental bad words.

-
HexShortID() 14 Chars

Standard lowercase hexadecimal encoded time-ordered short ID.

-

Human-Friendly Dated UIDs

HumanUid() 35 Chars

YYYYMMDD-HHMM-SSMM-MMMMNNNRRRRRRRRR formatted numeric ID.

-
NanoUid() 26 Chars

YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS-MMMMMM-NNN formatted time-prefixed ID.

-
MicroUid() 22 Chars

YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS-MMMMMM recommended for general usage.

-
SecUid() 15 Chars

YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS UTC timestamp ID to second precision.

-

Standard UUIDs

Uuid() / UuidV4() 36 Chars

Cryptographically strong random UUID v4.

-
UuidV1() 36 Chars

Time-based UUID version 1.

-
UuidV6() 36 Chars

Field-compatible reorder of UUID v1 for DB indexing.

-
UuidV7() 36 Chars

Unix Epoch time-based UUID version 7.

-
